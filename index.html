Privacy policy

This page describes the AgentLynk demo. AgentLynk answers messages that people send to the connected WhatsApp number.

What is stored

When you send a WhatsApp message to that number, the message text, the time it was sent, your WhatsApp number, and the reply are stored on the server that runs this demo. The stored messages are used to answer you and are shown to the person who operates this installation.

Who can see it

Only the operator of this installation can open the stored conversations. Messages are not sold.

Deleting your messages

To ask for your messages to be deleted, email a.maher010@gmail.com and include the WhatsApp number you wrote from. The operator will delete the stored conversation for that number.
